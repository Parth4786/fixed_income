# 7. The Eris curve is a reference, archived while it is free

Date: 2026-11-14

Status: Accepted

## Context

Anonymous access ends on 1 January 2027. It is also a modelled curve, so it is one referee among several, not the truth.

## Decision

Use the Eris end-of-day SOFR curve as an outside check, and download its history now.

## Consequences

About 600 MB of files that may not be redistributable; they stay out of the repository.
